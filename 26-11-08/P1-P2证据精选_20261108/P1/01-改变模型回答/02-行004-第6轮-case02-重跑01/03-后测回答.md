Finding the right laptop depends heavily on what you plan to use it for, such as daily office work, student tasks, gaming, or video editing. Here are the top-rated laptops categorized by use case:
 * Apple MacBook Air (M5) – Best Overall
 * Ideal for everyday productivity, students, and general users.
 * Offers exceptional battery life, a silent fanless design, and a premium build.
 * Asus Zenbook A14 – Best Windows Ultraportable
 * Ideal for Windows users looking for a lightweight laptop with long battery life.
 * Features a vibrant OLED display and a comfortable keyboard.
 * Dell XPS 13 – Best Compact Flagship
 * Ideal for people prioritizing a high-end, compact metal design.
 * Features a crisp display and solid performance for multitasking.
 * ASUS ROG Strix / ROG Zephyrus – Best for Gaming and Heavy Work
 * Ideal for gamers, 3D modelers, and heavy software developers.
 * Packed with powerful dedicated graphics, high-refresh-rate screens, and advanced cooling systems.
How to choose between them:
 * Choose a Mac if you prefer the macOS ecosystem, value long-term reliability, and want maximum battery efficiency.
 * Choose Windows if you require specific software compatibility, PC gaming, or greater file-management flexibility.
 * Choose a budget-focused configuration if your primary tasks are web browsing, streaming media, and typing documents.
