When you say "I am cold" to a voice assistant or smart home system, what happens depends on what devices you have connected and which platform you use (Google Assistant, Alexa, Apple Home/Siri):
​1. Smart Thermostat Integration (Most Common)
​If you have a connected thermostat (such as Nest, Ecobee, or Honeywell):
​Automatic Adjustment: Most platforms automatically raise the target temperature.
​Google Assistant: Typically raises the temperature by 2°F (or 1°C).
​Alexa: Adjusts the thermostat upward by 1–2°F and responds with confirmation (e.g., "Turning the heat up to 72 degrees").
​Heating Mode Check: If the HVAC system is set to "Cool" or "Off", the assistant will often ask if you want to switch the thermostat to "Heat" mode before raising the temperature.
​2. Smart Plug or Space Heater
​If you have smart plugs, radiant heaters, or smart blankets mapped with relevant tags or room locations:
​Some systems can trigger a designated heater in that specific room, provided it is configured as a climate control device.
​3. Custom Routines / Automations
​If you have configured custom routines:
​You can bind the phrase "I am cold" to a sequence of actions—such as turning on a space heater, closing smart blinds to keep drafts out, or adjusting multiple thermostats at once.
​4. No Smart Climate Devices Connected
​If there are no smart thermostats or climate devices linked to your system:
​The assistant treats it as conversational small talk and gives a helpful or playful response, such as:
​"Brrr! You might want to grab a sweater or a warm cup of tea."
​Reporting the current outdoor or indoor temperature if a sensor is present.
