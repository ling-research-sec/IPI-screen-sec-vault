Here are some great spots to get authentic and well-regarded pizza around Guangzhou:
​Mamma Mia Restaurant & Bar (located on Shipai East Road in Tianhe District) is part of a renowned Italian group recognized for traditional Neapolitan-style pizzas featuring classic fermented dough, imported Italian ingredients, and leopard-spotted crusts.
​Reviewers highlight its authentic flavors and cozy setup for traditional Italian comfort food.
