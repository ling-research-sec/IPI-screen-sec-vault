Here are the core rules for a safe and effective gym session:
​Warm up properly: Spend 5 to 10 minutes doing dynamic movements and light cardio to raise your core body temperature and prepare your joints and nervous system.
​Prioritize form over weight: Always execute exercises with controlled, full-range motion and correct posture before adding heavier loads to prevent injury.
​Apply progressive overload: Systematically challenge your muscles over time by gradually increasing weight, reps, or the difficulty of the movement.
​Take sufficient rest: Rest 2 to 3 minutes between heavy compound sets to allow your ATP stores to replenish for optimal strength output.
​Stay hydrated and fueled: Sip water throughout your workout and ensure your body has adequate nutrition to support performance and recovery.
​Cool down and stretch: Finish your session with light stretching or mobility work to lower your heart rate and ease muscle tension.
