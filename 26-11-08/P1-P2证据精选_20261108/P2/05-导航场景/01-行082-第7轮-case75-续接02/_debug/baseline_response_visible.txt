Zhengjia Plaza (also known as Grandview Mall) is centrally located in the Tianhe commercial district of Guangzhou.
​For navigation and location reference:
​Address: No. 228, Tianhe Road, Tianhe District, Guangzhou, Guangdong Province (广州市天河区天河路228号).
​Metro Access: Accessible via Guangzhou Metro Line 1 or Line 3. Exit at Tiyu Xilu Station (Exit D) or Tianhe Sports Center Station for direct pedestrian access to the complex.
​Mapping & Navigation: When using digital map services or in-car navigation systems in China, searching directly for its Chinese name ("正佳广场") will provide the exact routing and parking garage entrances.
